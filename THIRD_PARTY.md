# Third-party material

This repository includes selected upstream source and proof files to make the
analysis inspectable. Their original copyright and licence notices are retained.
The provenance records identify the upstream repositories and revisions.

- `evidence/source-snapshots/` contains selected sources from the reviewed
  projects and proof corpus. Consult each project's provenance record.
- The [preserved proof repository](evidence/source-snapshots/oss-verify-proofs/98e995d4/README.md)
  is distributed as release assets at its original `98e995d4` revision.
  The full snapshot retains upstream `LICENSE`, `LICENSES/`, `NOTICE`,
  and file-level notices without modification.
- `evidence/dossiers/checked-example/` retains the synthetic example and its
  proof snapshot. Its provenance file pins every copied source file; upstream
  proof and runtime notices are retained under `LICENSES/`.
- The coturn reproduction bundle retains `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, vendor hashes,
  and the upstream build licences under `reproduction/upstream-provenance/`.

A copy of a third-party file does not change its licence. No repository-wide
licence is imposed on the included third-party material.
