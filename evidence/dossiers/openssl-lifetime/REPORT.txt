VERDICT AND EXACT SCOPE

PASS: the internal bndiv lifetime predicate and operation contracts accept an
expired BN_CTX temporary. This remains true with the complete upstream state,
ownership and context conditions, not merely a weakened valid_ref surrogate.
The successful construction uses unchanged upstream definitions.

PASS: fresh compilation of the published abstract-client results
BnCorrect.fuzz_one_wp and BnCorrect.fuzz_one_rc, and their dependencies.
These are model-level results, not the final binary agreement certificate.

INCOMPLETE / NOT REPLAYED: asan_agrees_holds, ubsan_agrees_holds, and their
strong_universal_error_free_bndiv_* binary-level certificates. No claim about
their assumptions or successful replay is made here. Their source entry points
were inspected. The required LinuxProcessSemantics, stdpp, ReadFile, Flocq and
project image/statement libraries are absent from this local Rocq load path;
actual failing dependency probes are retained. The pinned bndiv tree has 1,591

WHAT WAS CHECKED

1. All 28 compiled upstream modules are byte-identical to the pinned Git blobs:
   18 bndiv modules in Spec/ and 10 bignum modules in BgSpec/. Four audit modules
   in exp/ were compiled from source. The final fresh build passed all 32 files.
   The original audit directories and all paper files were left untouched.

2. exp/LegacyStaleRef.v is an unchanged COPY of the previously supplied audit
   source; none of its previous logs or compiled objects was reused. Its three
   lemmas establish validity after end, acceptance of start/get/end/set-negative,
   and positional reuse of the same reference on the next start/get. In
   exp/Assumptions.v the stale-write theorem is explicitly instantiated at
   upstream ls_init with empty ownership using BnCorrect.ls_inv_init.

3. exp/IndependentWitness.v is a newly written, independent concrete witness.
   It imports the actual upstream modules, rather than redefining their logic.
   Starting at ls_init, its synthetic client executes:

       BN_CTX_start;
       t := BN_CTX_get;
       BN_bin2bn [1] t;
       BN_CTX_end;
       BN_set_negative t true.

   Upstream run_tree computes three distinct successful allocations:
   (128 bytes, id 1), (400 bytes, id 2), (8 bytes, id 3), and no free events.
   The temporary is RPool 0, with value +1 before frame end. The checked state
   after end has cx_frames = [], cx_used = cx_pused = 0, and a retained pool of
   16 entries. RPool 0 still satisfies valid_ref. st_ok, own, and ctx_rest all
   hold. The owned blocks are [(3,8); (2,400); (1,128)].

   all_end_preconditions proves every hypothesis of BN_CTX_end_spec.
   all_setter_preconditions_after_end proves every hypothesis of
   BN_set_negative_spec, plus ctx_rest and the empty-frame/zero-used facts.
   stale_write_accepted_by_exact_setter_spec invokes the unmodified setter
   lemma, establishing st_ok and own afterward. The sign actually changes
   from false to true, while frames remain empty and used remains zero.
   entire_synthetic_client_executes checks the complete concrete trace by
   reflexivity. nonempty_pool_and_no_active_frame is an explicit existential
   witness, avoiding contradictory or vacuous invariant assumptions.

4. The relevant upstream definitions and conditions were inspected in full:
   - BnInv.v: valid_ref (RPool i) requires i < 3 and nonempty cx_vals; st_ok
     constrains the five fixed BIGNUMs and first three pool BIGNUMs; own is
     a permutation of the retained allocated blocks.
   - BnModel.v: BN_CTX_end pops the frame and resets usage, retaining cx_vals.
     BN_set_negative reads and writes through getbn/setbn.
   - BnSpecs.v: start/get/end require the stated context discipline. ctx_wf
     allows a populated pool with no active frame and used = 0. The setter
     requires st_ok, own and valid_ref, but no active-frame condition.
   - BnMInv.v was also built and inspected: valid_refm uses the pool-list
     length, and likewise is not a lifetime/generation test. The new witness
     does not claim a machine-level certificate against this separate layer.

5. Published-client scope was inspected in BnModel.v. bn_div_fixed_top obtains
   tmp/snum/sdiv, uses them, and ends with BN_CTX_end (lines 417–455).
   bn_mul_fixed_top similarly uses its temporary before its final end
   (lines 471–515). fuzz_one uses fixed RB1–RB5 references. The fresh published
   fuzz_one_wp proof succeeds. This audit provides no reachable expired access
   by the actual published client and no falsification of its agreement theorem.

6. Separate bignum scope: the retained, upstream-verified BgInv.v defines
   live r s as r < NFIX + gx_used (st_ctx s). A fresh replay of exp/BgStale.v
   checks that start/get/end makes the temporary non-live; a second start/get
   reuses the same positional reference. Both results are axiom-free.
   This supports the stated contrast: bignum has a stronger immediate liveness
   guard. It is not a claim that every OpenSSL proof lacks liveness, nor a full
   verification of bignum's callers or generational provenance.

These are statements about the supplied mathematical models. The connection
between an expired temporary and an API-contract violation uses the pinned
OpenSSL manual, which states that BN_CTX_get pointers become invalid when
BN_CTX_end is called. The C implementation retains the backing pool. Neither
the witness nor that API contract establishes a free-based dangling pointer,
a sanitizer finding, an exploit, or a bug in the published client's execution.
The full binary model, lifting, sanitizer equivalence and final certificate
assumptions are outside this replay.

This is therefore a successful source replay under the local 9.0.0 kernel,
not an official-checker/environment reproduction. No upstream source edits or

REPRODUCTION

With Rocq/coqc, coqdep and coqchk installed, run from this artifact directory:

  python3 verify_sources.py
  ROCQ_BIN=/Users/umang/.opam/default/bin python3 replay.py

ROCQ_BIN may instead point at another installation's bin directory. replay.py
removes only compiled products within its own Spec/, BgSpec/ and exp/ directories,
computes dependency order with coqdep, builds every source with coqc, and runs
coqchk. It overwrites its own logs; keep a copy of the supplied logs if needed.
No network access is needed to replay the retained sources.

The coqc load-path flags are:
  -Q Spec OssFuzzTheorems.Proofs.openssl.bndiv.lib.Spec
  -Q BgSpec OssFuzzTheorems.Proofs.openssl.bignum.lib.Spec
  -Q exp Replay

logs/build-results.json records exact commands and exit codes;
logs/dependency-order.txt and logs/bignum-dependency-order.txt record ordering.
Each compilation has its own fresh log. IndependentWitness-development.log is
an earlier successful development check; the final fresh-build logs are
IndependentWitness.log and Assumptions.log, both successful.
fetch_selected.py and fetch_provenance.py preserve the exact raw-source URLs.
The Git tree request URLs are included in their saved JSON responses.

are deliberately excluded from the distributable tar archive and must be rebuilt.

MINIMAL PAPER WORDING

The central local-predicate claim is supported. Suggested precise wording:

  "At revision 98e995d4, bndiv's internal valid_ref predicate and
  BN_set_negative_spec admit a pool reference after BN_CTX_end, even when the
  upstream state, ownership and context invariants hold. We checked this in
  Rocq against the unchanged definitions, including a concrete synthetic
  start/get/initialize/end/write execution from ls_init. Thus these local
  contracts alone do not enforce the documented BN_CTX temporary lifetime.
  This does not demonstrate an invalid access in the published bndiv client,
  whose modeled temporaries are used before their frame ends, or invalidate
  its binary agreement theorem. The separate bignum proof has a stronger
  liveness predicate."

For the currently inspected sentence "valid_ref cannot support full memory
safety", the minimal correction is "valid_ref and these local operation
contracts alone do not establish the documented temporary-lifetime property".
Additional caller-specific structure can enforce a property absent from a local
predicate. Avoid presenting an internal proof lemma as a promised reusable API,
and avoid equating the synthetic model witness with a counterexample to the
full published binary certificate. Update the methodology, if desired, to say
that this independent fresh Rocq replay now supplements the earlier source
inspection. No paper edits were made by this chat.
